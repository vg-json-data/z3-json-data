# Code of Conduct

Be respectful to everyone. Bigotry and hate speech will not be tolerated.

Maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate this standard and may restrict or ban participants when appropriate. Responses will depend on the nature and severity of the behavior; a warning may be appropriate for an isolated or minor incident, while serious or repeated misconduct may result in an immediate ban.

If you encounter a serious issue, please contact the maintainers.
